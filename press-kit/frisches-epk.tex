\section*{Quick Take}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.58\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
Frisches is a four-piece band from Munich shaped by guitar drive, vocal and flute interplay, and a rhythm section that plays with weight rather than excess. The project works best when presented as both a sonic and visual act: dark surfaces, warm metallic accents, intimate rehearsal energy, and songs that feel staged without becoming theatrical.

The current release, \textit{Tales From The Cellar}, gives journalists and bookers an easy entry point: a tight set of character-rich songs, a recognisable line-up, and enough visual material to support listings, features, previews, and social promotion.

\vspace{0.75em}

\begin{frcard}
  \textbf{Line-up}\\[0.25em]
  Cami on vocals and flute, Edgar on guitar and backing vocals, Tobi on bass, and Steff on drums.
\end{frcard}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.38\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\begin{frcard}
  \textbf{At a glance}\\[0.3em]
  Base: Munich, Germany\\
  Setup: four-piece live band\\
  Release focus: \textit{Tales From The Cellar}\\
  Best fit: club shows, support slots, press coverage, visual editorials
\end{frcard}
